Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026. This policy applies to the SISR macOS application and related documentation sites published for the project.

Summary

SISR does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Photo sequences you open are processed on your Mac. The app does not require an account and does not phone home with analytics or advertising identifiers.

Data the app processes

  • Image files you select — read from folders you authorize in order to preview and encode video or GIF output.
  • Embedded metadata (for example EXIF capture dates) — read locally when you enable date overlays or when the UI shows capture time.
  • Project preferences — settings such as recent folders (security-scoped bookmarks), last-used encode options, and window layout may be stored in standard macOS preferences / application support on your device.

Output videos and GIFs are written only to locations you choose. We do not upload your media to any SISR server — there is no SISR cloud service for your files.

Permissions

On macOS, SISR may request access to folders you open (App Sandbox / security-scoped bookmarks). Those permissions are managed by the system and can be revoked in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Internet use

The application itself does not require a network connection to open sequences or render. Distribution sites (GitHub Releases, this documentation site, or the Mac App Store if listed) may log standard web/server analytics under those hosts’ policies — not under this app’s control.

Children

SISR is a general-purpose media utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Open source

Source code is published at github.com/timelapsetech/SISR-MacOS under the MIT License. You can inspect what the app does with local files.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. Material changes will be reflected in the repository documentation for the release that ships them.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see Support, or open an issue on GitHub.
